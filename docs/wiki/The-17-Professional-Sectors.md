# The 17 Specialized Professional Sectors in Bhashabid

Bhashabid provides authoritative stylistic, lexical, and syntactic standards across **17 specialized professional sectors**, organized into 9 modular progressive reference guides.

---

## 1. Constitutional Law & High Court Writs
- **Authoritative Standards**: Constitution of Bangladesh (Article 102), Supreme Court precedents, statutory petition taxonomy.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Show-cause orders (Rule Nisi): `"[বিষয়বস্তু] কেন [আইনগত ফলাফল] ঘোষণা করা হবে না, তা জানতে চেয়ে রুল জারি করেছেন হাইকোর্ট।"`
  - Avoid literal English calques: Never use *"জিজ্ঞেস করে রুল জারি করেছে"*.
  - Statutory remedies: Distinct prayers for *Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Certiorari, Prohibition, Quo Warranto*.
- **Reference**: [`constitutional_writs.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/constitutional_writs.md)

## 2. Macroeconomics & Banking Journalism
- **Authoritative Standards**: Bangladesh Bank Monetary Policy Statements, prudential regulations, The Business Standard (TBS) verified style.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Financial terminology: `নীতি সুদহার` (Policy rate), `বেসিস পয়েন্ট` (Basis points), `মূল্যস্ফীতি` (Inflation), `মুদ্রানীতি` (Monetary policy).
  - Percentages in prose: Use **শতাংশ** (`১০.৮৭ শতাংশ`) instead of `%` signs in formal sentences.
  - Active causal structure: Prefer `মূল্যবৃদ্ধির কারণে... দাঁড়িয়েছে` over passive calques (`মূল্যবৃদ্ধির দ্বারা চালিত`).
- **Reference**: [`journalism_domains.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/journalism_domains.md)

---

## 3. Administrative Governance & Secretariat Petitions
- **Authoritative Standards**: *Bangladesh Secretariat Instructions* (*সচিবালয় নির্দেশমালা, জনপ্রশাসন মন্ত্রণালয়*).
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Formal salutations: `বরাবর, [পদবি], [দপ্তর], [ঠিকানা]` ➔ `বিষয়:` ➔ `জনাব / মহোদয়,` (Never 'প্রিয় মহাশয়' or 'সম্মানিত স্যার').
  - Formal opening: `বিনীত নিবেদন এই যে, ...`
  - Formal closing prayer: `অতএব, মহোদয়ের সমীপে বিনীত প্রার্থনা...` ➔ `বিনীত / নিবেদক` (Never 'আপনার বিশ্বস্তভাবে').
- **Reference**: [`official_letters.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/official_letters.md)

## 4. Epistolary Hierarchy & Formal Letter Writing
- **Authoritative Standards**: Traditional and contemporary Bengali administrative correspondence protocols.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - To Seniors/Teachers: `শ্রদ্ধেয় / শ্রদ্ধেয়া` ➔ `প্রণামান্তে / আপনার স্নেহধন্য`
  - To Peers/Friends: `সুপ্রিয় / প্রিয়` ➔ `ইতি / তোমারই বন্ধু`
  - To Juniors: `স্নেহের` ➔ `শুভাকাঙ্ক্ষী`
  - Professional: `সুধী / মহোদয়` ➔ `ধন্যবাদান্তে / বিনীত`
- **Reference**: [`official_letters.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/official_letters.md)

## 5. Creative Fiction & Living Spoken Dialogue
- **Authoritative Standards**: Bangla Academy Standard Bengali Grammar (*প্রমিত বাংলা ভাষার ব্যাকরণ*).
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Living spoken Cholito: Authentic conversational register in quotes (`“তুই কি সত্যি বলছিস?”`).
  - Action-embedded speech tags: Replace repetitive *"তিনি বললেন যে..."* with character actions (`চায়ের কাপে চুমুক দিয়ে রাতুল জানালার বাইরে তাকাল...`).
  - Sensory imagery and emotional cadence.
- **Reference**: [`creative_writing.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/creative_writing.md)

## 6. Classical Poetry & Prosodic Scansion
- **Authoritative Standards**: Rabindranath Tagore and Probodh Chandra Sen Bengali Prosody (*ছন্দতত্ত্ব*).
- **Key Invariants**:
  - **Dalabritta** (দলবৃত্ত): 1 mora per syllable (open and closed).
  - **Matrabritta** (মাত্রাবৃত্ত): Open syllable = 1 mora, closed syllable = 2 morae.
  - **Aksharbritta / Payar** (অক্ষরবৃত্ত / পয়ার): 14 akshars partitioned into 8 + 6 couplets with caesura harmony.
- **Reference**: [`creative_writing.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/creative_writing.md)

## 7. Healthcare, Clinical Advisories & Epidemiology
- **Authoritative Standards**: Bangla Academy Medical Science Dictionary (*চিকিৎসাবিজ্ঞান পরিভাষা*), DGHS guidelines.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Symptom vs. sign: `উপসর্গ` (Symptoms reported by patient) vs `লক্ষণ` (Objective clinical signs observed by physician).
  - Explicit dosage intervals: `প্রতি ৮ ঘণ্টা পর পর ১টি করে ট্যাবলেট ভরা পেটে সেব্য`.
  - Non-stigmatizing medical terminology: Never translate clinical 'drug' into `মাদক` (narcotic); use `ওষুধ / অ্যান্টিবায়োটিক`.
- **Reference**: [`academic_legal_medical.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/academic_legal_medical.md)

## 8. Land Registry & Deeds Conveyancing
- **Authoritative Standards**: Transfer of Property Act 1882, Registration Act 1908, Ministry of Land official protocols.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Deed types: `সাফ-কবলা` (Outright sale), `হেবা-বিল-এওয়াজ` (Gift deed), `বণ্টননামা` (Partition deed).
  - Survey chain: Strict taxonomy of `সিএস (CS)`, `এসএ (SA)`, `আরএস (RS)`, `বিএস (BS)` খতিয়ান, দাগ নং, মৌজা, চৌহদ্দি (boundaries), and নামজারি (mutation).
- **Reference**: [`academic_legal_medical.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/academic_legal_medical.md)

## 9. Agronomy & Disaster Early Warning
- **Authoritative Standards**: Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE), Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) Standing Orders on Disaster (SOD).
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Official maritime storm warnings: `মহাবিপদ সংকেত ১-১০` (Great Danger Signal), `বিপদ সংকেত` (Danger Signal), `স্থানীয় সতর্ক সংকেত`.
  - Storm surge: Standardized as `জলোচ্ছ্বাস` (Never calque as *"ঝড় বৃদ্ধি"*).
  - Agronomic inputs: `সুষম সার ও বালাইনাশক প্রয়োগ`.
- **Reference**: [`academic_legal_medical.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/academic_legal_medical.md)

## 10. ICT, Unicode Standards & Software Engineering
- **Authoritative Standards**: Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC) Bengali Computing Standards, Unicode Consortium NFC specifications.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Canonical NFC decomposition of Bengali grapheme clusters (`ক্ষ`, `জ্ঞ`, `হ্ণ`, `হ্ম`).
  - Dari (`।`) enforcement while strictly protecting ASCII decimals in technical floats (`৩.১৪`).
  - Open-source localization best practices without awkward English jargon.
- **Reference**: [`orthography.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/orthography.md)

## 11. Education & NCTB Creative Pedagogy
- **Authoritative Standards**: National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB) Standard Bengali Pedagogy.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - NCTB 4-tier Bloom's taxonomy rubric:
    1. `উদ্দীপক` (Contextual stimulus)
    2. `(ক) জ্ঞানমূলক প্রশ্ন` (Knowledge: 1 mark)
    3. `(খ) অনুধাবনমূলক প্রশ্ন` (Comprehension: 2 marks)
    4. `(গ) প্রয়োগমূলক প্রশ্ন` (Application: 3 marks)
    5. `(ঘ) উচ্চতর দক্ষতামূলক প্রশ্ন` (Higher Order Thinking: 4 marks)
- **Reference**: [`academic_legal_medical.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/academic_legal_medical.md)

## 12. Translation, Calque De-Naturalization & Idioms
- **Authoritative Standards**: Bangla Academy Standard Bengali Grammar, Proverbs & Idioms canon.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Passive de-calquing: Eliminate unidiomatic *"দ্বারা"* constructions.
  - Cultural proverb equivalence:
    - *A bad workman blames his tools* ➔ `নাচতে না জানলে উঠান বাঁকা`
    - *To beat around the bush* ➔ `ঘুরিয়ে-পেঁচিয়ে কথা বলা`
    - *Spill the beans* ➔ `হাঁড়ি ভেঙে দেওয়া`
  - Tri-layer semantics: Distinguish *অভিধা* (literal), *লক্ষণা* (metaphoric), and *ব্যঞ্জনা* (suggestive).
- **Reference**: [`grammar_registers.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/grammar_registers.md)

## 13. Consumer E-Commerce & Retail Logistics *(NEW)*
- **Authoritative Standards**: Consumers' Rights Protection Act 2009 (*ভোক্তা অধিকার সংরক্ষণ আইন ২০০৯*), e-CAB Operational Guidelines.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Honorific concordance in transaction updates:
    - `তারা সেই অর্থ ফেরত দিয়েছিল` (Standard)
    - `তাঁরা সেই অর্থ ফেরত দিয়েছিলেন` (Honorific)
    - Never mix informal pronoun with honorific verb (*তারা ... দিয়েছিলেন* ❌).
  - Delivery dispatch, parcel conveyancing, return window, and escrow dispute resolution terminology.
- **Reference**: [`ecommerce_fintech_civic.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/ecommerce_fintech_civic.md)

## 14. Customer Support & Omnichannel Messaging *(NEW)*
- **Authoritative Standards**: Telecom & Omnichannel Customer Experience Quality Benchmarks, Bangla Academy Conversational Polite Register Standards.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Administrative resolution nomenclature: Use `টিকিট`, `সমাধান`, `নিষ্পত্তি`, `পর্যালোচনা`, `প্রতিনিধি নির্ধারণ`.
  - Eliminate hybrid Banglish verbs: Avoid `রিজল্ভ`, `ক্লোজ`, `সল্ভ`, `এসাইন`.
  - Alphanumeric tracking code preservation: Keep ticket identifiers intact (`TCK-942`, `Ticket ID: TCK-881`).
- **Reference**: [`ecommerce_fintech_civic.md`](../../skills/bangla-expert/references/ecommerce_fintech_civic.md)

## 17. Mass Media & Digital Journalism *(NEW)*
- **Authoritative Standards**: Bangladesh Press Council Code of Ethics & Stylebook, Press Institute of Bangladesh (PIB) Reporting Standards.
- **Key Invariants**:
  - Neutral attribution: `তিনি বলেন`, `বলে জানা গেছে`, `সূত্র নিশ্চিত করেছে`.
  - Avoid sensationalist adjectives or unverified claims in hard news reporting.
  - Consistent capitalization and transcript formatting for press briefings and interviews.
