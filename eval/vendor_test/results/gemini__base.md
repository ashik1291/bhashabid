## V08

In natural Bengali, time is expressed using traditional fractional terms (**পৌনে** for "quarter to" and **সাড়ে** for "half past") along with a time-of-day marker rather than AM/PM.

* **3:45 PM:** **বিকেল পৌনে চারটা** (*Bikel poune charta*)
* **বিকেল** (*Bikel*) = Afternoon (used for PM hours from roughly 3:00 PM to 5:59 PM)
* **পৌনে চারটা** (*Poune charta*) = Quarter to four

* **2:30 PM:** **দুপুর আড়াইটা** (*Dupur arait-a*)
* **দুপুর** (*Dupur*) = Midday/afternoon (used for PM hours from roughly 12:00 PM to 2:59 PM)
* **আড়াইটা** (*Arait-a*) = Two-thirty (a unique single word used specifically for 2:30 instead of saying *saare duita*)

## Natural Conversational Phrasing

**মুসলধারে বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল, তাই খেলাটা বাতিল করা হয়েছে।**

* **Idiom Equivalent:** **"মুসলধারে বৃষ্টি" (musholdhare brishti)** literally means "rain in streams thick as a pestle" and is the standard, natural Bengali equivalent for "raining cats and dogs."
* **Everyday Vocabulary:** Uses **খেলাটা (khelata)** ("the game/match") and **বাতিল করা হয়েছে (batil kora hoyechhe)** ("was canceled/called off")

## Formal / News Broadcast Style

**প্রবল বর্ষণের কারণে ম্যাচটি পরিত্যক্ত ঘোষণা করা হয়েছে।**

* **Official Sports Terminology:** Sports broadcasts and news reports typically use **ম্যাচটি (match-ti)** ("the match") and **পরিত্যক্ত (porityokto)** ("abandoned/called off")
